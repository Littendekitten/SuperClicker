Sushi Factory

Buy pieces in the Shop. Pick one in the hotbar (or press 1-9), click a square, and press R to turn it. Rearrange your hotbar in the Inventory. Rice cookers make rice balls. Chopping tables are little factories: place one, then use ✏️ Edit on it to build inside.
